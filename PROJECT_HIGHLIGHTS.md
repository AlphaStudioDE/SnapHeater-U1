# SnapHeater U1 — highlights

**Your chamber heater. Connected to your print.**

0.9.9 brings original Panda Breath hardware, an Android companion and direct
Snapmaker U1/Moonraker integration together in an open, printer-aware workflow.

- Prepare the chamber with preheat/hold.
- Let AUTO follow the printer and optionally temper after completion.
- Keep accepted jobs running locally when the phone disconnects.
- Organize multiple heaters with friendly names.
- Inspect charts and events, recover recent history gaps and export reports.
- Optionally supervise supported printer chamber ventilation through Symbiont.
- Update the inactive application slot with image and SHA-256 validation.

This is a **supervised testing prerelease**, not a certified/stable product.
There is no proven print-quality, energy-saving or hardware-safety superiority claim.
See [README](README.md), [feature status](FEATURE_MATRIX.md) and
[the project story](docs/PROJECT_STORY.md).
